# Product vision

**Owner:** Martin · **Last reviewed:** 2026-11-13

## Vision

Make it easy and safe to share the empty seats in cars already travelling
around Iceland, so that getting between towns is affordable for everyone and
fewer cars carry one person.

## The problem

- Intercity public transport in Iceland is sparse. Many routes have one or two
  buses a day, some only in summer.
- Many people have no car or prefer not to drive long distances in winter:
  students, young people, residents of smaller towns, foreign workers and
  visitors.
- Drivers make the same trips with empty seats and pay the full cost alone.
- Today's informal options (Facebook groups, message boards, asking around) are
  hard to search, have no booking or seat tracking, and give little sense of
  who you are travelling with.

## Who it is for

| Persona | Situation | What they need |
| ------- | --------- | -------------- |
| **Commuting driver** | Drives Selfoss to Reykjavík every weekday | Post a recurring trip in seconds, predictable passengers, cost sharing |
| **Long-distance driver** | Drives Reykjavík to Akureyri a few times a year | Fill seats along the way, not only end to end |
| **Student or young adult** | Goes home for the weekend, no car | Cheap, trustworthy rides on a few common routes |
| **Resident of a small town** | Needs to get to the capital for appointments | Rides from a nearby meeting point, on a specific date |
| **Foreign worker** | New to Iceland, may not speak Icelandic | English (and later Polish) interface, no need for an Icelandic ID |
| **Visitor** | Travels without a rental car | Rides from the airport and between sights, clear meeting points, safety information |
| **Operator (Martin)** | Runs the service alone | Automated checks, a clear admin console, low operating effort |

## What makes ShareRide different

1. **Meeting points, not addresses.** Trips are built from curated, safe, easy
   to find meeting points. Drivers don't detour to doorsteps; passengers know
   exactly where to stand, even in winter darkness.
2. **Partial routes.** A passenger can ride any part of a trip. One seat can
   carry Reykjavík to Borgarnes for one person and Borgarnes to Akureyri for
   another.
3. **Fair, transparent prices.** Each segment has its own price, worked out
   from the real cost of driving it; the driver only chooses one price level,
   or a free ride. The passenger sees exactly what each part costs.
4. **Built for Iceland.** Icelandic and English, weather and road warnings,
   112 and SafeTravel information, works for residents and visitors alike.

## How we will know it works

| Metric | Why it matters | Beta target (confirmed 2026-11-13) |
| ------ | -------------- | ------------------------ |
| Searches with at least one result | Liquidity: the core health signal | 50 percent on launch corridors |
| Search to booking request | Do results match what people need? | 20 percent of searches with results |
| Seat fill rate (booked seat-km / offered seat-km) | Value created per trip | 30 percent |
| Driver cancellation rate, excluding weather | Reliability and trust | Under 10 percent |
| Repeat bookings within 60 days | Retention | 30 percent of passengers |
| Safety reports per 1 000 trips | Safety | Tracked; every report handled within 24 hours |

## Out of scope

- On-demand rides (Uber style). Drivers publish trips they are making anyway.
- Door-to-door pickups outside published meeting points.
- Professional drivers or commercial passenger transport.
- Freight and parcel delivery (a possible later idea, not planned).

## Competitive landscape (to research in Phase 1)

- **Facebook groups and informal boards** for shared rides: free and familiar,
  but no search, booking, seat tracking or verification.
- **Samferða** and similar Icelandic ride boards: established names among
  travellers; check current activity.
- **Strætó** intercity buses and airport coaches: scheduled and reliable, but
  limited frequency and coverage.
- **International ride-share platforms:** to our knowledge none operates in
  Iceland today; verify in Phase 1.
- **Rental cars and taxis:** comfortable but expensive for one or two people.

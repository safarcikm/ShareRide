Small animations

A moving preview for spec 33. The same car, puffins and Iceland scene as the app, drawn from its shapes and colours, in your light or dark mode. Pick how the front page's banner moves; the rest are my proposals.

Your device asks for less motion, so this page starts with Reduce motion on: everything stands still, as it would for you on PuffinRide. Turn the switch off to see the motion.

1. The front page's banner

Once a visit, the first time Find shows. The words and the search work from the first frame; only the drawing moves.

A on a laptop

The car stands on the hills beside the title, so it rolls in over the last stretch and fades in as it comes, never passing under the words.

2. A booked seat, and a request sent

The title and the steps are there at once; the drawing never holds them back.

3. The road while offering a ride

Press Next or Back: the car rolls to the new step in 400 ms and the moss line grows with it. On the booking page the road stands still.

4. Small touches

Choosing seats

The seat just added pops, in 150 ms. On a phone, a tick with each step.

1 seats

A menu opening

Opens with a fade and an 8-point move out of its button, in 180 ms; closes with a fade in 120 ms. With Reduce motion on, it only fades.

Taps on a phone

Felt, not seen, so this page cannot play them. Only in the apps, always with something on screen, one per press, and off when the phone's own setting is off.

5. What stays still

  • Switching tabs, and ride cards coming into a list: seen many times a day.
  • Prices, times, seat counts and names: never on money or facts.
  • Anything that loops: no shimmering placeholders, no car that keeps driving (option D shows why).
  • Scrolling effects, confetti, sounds, a moving logo, animated emails, the map.
  • On the website, pages change at once. On phones, screens change the way the phone does it, as today.

A banner for every season

A moving preview for spec 36. The front page's scene follows the year in Iceland: four seasons, and the big days with something of their own on the car. Each day's moment plays once, with the car's arrival, and then it all stands still. Nothing here is built yet.

Your device asks for less motion, so this page starts with Reduce motion on: everything stands still, as it would for you on PuffinRide. Turn the switch off to see the motion.

8. How it behaves

  • The car's arrival stays as it is: it drives in, dips, and the puffins hop, once a visit (spec 33).
  • A day's moment starts with the arrival and is over within 3.5 seconds. Nothing loops, and only the drawing moves: the search works from the first frame.
  • With Reduce motion on, the scene shows the day's look standing still: the tree lit, the flags out, the egg's chick on top.
  • Coming back to Find in the same visit, the scene shows the day's look, still.
  • The day is Iceland's date. It changes at midnight in Reykjavík, worked out on the device; nothing is sent anywhere.
  • The greeting is real text in Icelandic or English, so screen readers read it; the drawing stays hidden from them, as today.
  • One switch in the console turns the days off, for a sad day when a party would be wrong. The seasons stay.
  • Left out unless you want them: Valentine's Day, Halloween, Culture Night, Pride, 1 May, and the religious days besides Christmas and Easter.

# Spec 13: Payments, refunds, payouts and receipts

**Owner:** Martin · **Date:** 2026-11-13 · **Status:** Approved (2026-11-13)

Features: D7 Pay in the app, D8 Receipts, C8 Payouts to drivers, H7 Payments,
refunds and payouts in the console ([features](../product/features.md)).
Related: [ADR 0013](../decisions/0013-in-app-payments-only.md),
[pricing and seats](../product/pricing-and-seats.md#payment-and-refunds)
(rules M1 to M5), [legal and regulatory](../compliance/legal-and-regulatory.md#5-payments),
[spec 5](2026-11-13-booking-and-requests.md) (when a booking is paid).

## 1. Problem and goal

Every booking with a price is paid in the app (ADR 0013). Passengers must be
able to pay in seconds with a card or their phone's wallet, get refunds without
asking, and receive proper receipts. Drivers must be paid reliably without
ShareRide ever handling their bank details. The operator, working alone, must
be able to see where every króna is and fix problems from the console.

Goal: a licensed payment provider takes passengers' payments, holds the money,
refunds it when the rules say so, and pays drivers 24 hours after each trip.
ShareRide keeps an exact record of every money movement and never touches card
or bank details.

## 2. User stories

- As a passenger, I pay with Apple Pay, Google Pay or a card in a few seconds,
  and get a receipt by email.
- As a passenger, I cancel in time and see the full refund on its way without
  contacting anyone.
- As a driver, I set up payouts once and get paid automatically after each
  trip, and I can see what is coming and what has been paid.
- As a driver, I know a late cancellation or a no-show still pays me.
- As the operator, I can see every payment, refund and payout, refund a booking
  by hand, hold a payout when a problem is reported, and export the month for
  the accountant.

## 3. Behaviour

### 3.1 The payment provider

The provider is chosen in its own ADR after talks with Rapyd, Teya and Straumur
(and, if none fits, an EEA company using Stripe or Adyen). It must:

1. Hold passengers' money under its own licence and pay it out to drivers
   (a marketplace or platform product), so ShareRide needs no payment licence
   of its own (to be confirmed by the legal opinion).
2. Take cards (Visa and Mastercard at least), Apple Pay and Google Pay, in the
   app and on the web, with strong customer authentication (3-D Secure).
3. Refund in full or in part, through an API.
4. Check drivers' identity and bank accounts itself (hosted onboarding) and pay
   out to Icelandic bank accounts in ISK.
5. Report every event by signed webhooks, offer a test mode, and process data
   in the EEA under a data processing agreement.

Everything provider-specific sits behind the `PaymentProvider` port, so the
provider can change without touching the domain.

### 3.2 Paying (passenger)

1. The payment step in spec 5 (3.5) opens the provider's payment sheet with the
   amount in ISK: Apple Pay or Google Pay where available, otherwise a card
   form from the provider. The provider handles 3-D Secure.
2. The amount is captured at once, not only authorised, because trips can be
   up to 60 days ahead and authorisations do not last that long. Refunds handle
   cancellations.
3. The provider can save the card for next time if the passenger agrees; only
   the provider's token, card brand and last four digits are kept.
4. The booking is confirmed when the provider's signed confirmation arrives,
   not when the app says it succeeded.
5. If confirmation arrives after the payment window has closed, the booking is
   not revived: the payment is refunded in full automatically.
6. Card fees are paid by ShareRide in the beta (rule M4): the passenger pays the
   booking price and the driver receives all of it.

### 3.3 Refunds

1. Refunds follow rule M2 and start automatically when a booking is cancelled
   (cancellation flows are spec 6):
   - passenger cancels 15 minutes or more before boarding: full refund;
   - driver cancels the booking or the trip: full refund;
   - departure moved more than 30 minutes and the passenger cancels: full
     refund;
   - passenger cancels later, or is a no-show: no refund.
2. The passenger sees "Full refund of {amount} on its way. Your bank may take a
   few days." and gets a refund receipt.
3. Refunds go to the original payment method. A failed refund is retried and,
   if it still fails, appears in the operator's queue.
4. The operator can also refund in full or in part with a reason (3.6).

### 3.4 Payouts (driver)

1. **Set up:** before publishing a trip with a price above 0, the driver sets up
   payouts through the provider's hosted onboarding (identity and bank
   account), decision 1. Free trips need no set-up. The driver readiness
   checklist in spec 3 gains this item for priced trips.
2. ShareRide stores only the provider's account reference and its status: not
   started, pending, verified, needs attention or rejected.
3. **When:** 24 hours after the trip completes (rule M3), the driver is paid the
   price of every confirmed booking that was completed, marked as a no-show, or
   cancelled late by the passenger.
4. **Held:** a booking whose passenger has reported a problem (spec 9) within
   those 24 hours is held until the operator releases it or refunds it.
5. **Earnings screen:** upcoming payouts by trip, paid payouts with dates,
   held amounts with the reason, and the payout account's status. A yearly
   statement can be downloaded for tax purposes.

### 3.5 Receipts

1. Every payment and refund produces a receipt, by email and in the booking:
   receipt number, date, the company's name, address and registration number,
   booking reference, trip date and part, seats, amount in ISK, card brand and
   last four digits, and the driver's first name.
2. The wording ("contribution to travel costs, paid through ShareRide") and any
   VAT lines are confirmed with the accountant and the legal opinion before the
   beta.
3. Receipts are in the passenger's app language.

### 3.6 Operator console

1. Payments list with filters (status, date, trip, user), and each booking's
   money timeline: payment, refunds, payout.
2. Refund a booking in full or in part, with a reason recorded in the audit log.
3. Hold and release payouts; see held payouts and why.
4. Chargebacks: the provider's notice appears with its deadline; the operator
   answers with the booking's evidence (times, status, check-ins). Chat
   messages are only used when a report has been filed, as in the privacy
   rules. A lost chargeback is taken from the driver's next payouts if the
   trip did not happen because of the driver; otherwise ShareRide carries it
   in the beta (decision 2, rule M5).
5. Daily reconciliation: our ledger is compared with the provider's report, and
   any difference is listed for the operator.
6. Monthly export of payments, refunds, payouts and fees for the accountant.

### 3.7 Failures

- **Webhooks** are verified by signature, processed once (by event ID), and may
  arrive late or out of order; the ledger only moves forward.
- **Provider down:** the payment sheet shows "Payments are not available right
  now. Try again in a few minutes."; the booking stays accepted until its
  window ends.
- **Payout fails** (for example a closed bank account): the payout is held, the
  driver is asked to update their account, and it is retried once fixed.
- **Driver's payout account rejected or needs attention:** their priced trips
  stay published and payouts accumulate, held, until it is fixed; the operator
  is alerted.

## 4. Rules

Rules M1 to M5 in [pricing and seats](../product/pricing-and-seats.md#payment-and-refunds)
apply. This spec adds:

| ID | Rule |
| -- | ---- |
| R13.1 | All money is in ISK, stored as whole krónur with the currency code. |
| R13.2 | ShareRide never stores or sees card numbers or bank account details; the provider holds them. |
| R13.3 | A booking is confirmed only by the provider's signed confirmation. A payment confirmed after the window closed is refunded in full. |
| R13.4 | Payments are captured at once; cancellations are handled by refunds. |
| R13.5 | Every money movement (payment, refund, payout, fee, chargeback) is an entry in an append-only ledger; for each booking the entries balance once it is settled. |
| R13.6 | Payouts are made 24 hours after trip completion for confirmed bookings that were completed, no-shows or late cancellations; a reported problem holds that booking's payout. |
| R13.7 | Publishing a trip with a price needs a payout account that is pending or verified (decision 1). |
| R13.8 | Webhook events are verified and processed exactly once. |
| R13.9 | Operator refunds, payout holds and releases need a reason and are written to the audit log. |
| R13.10 | Receipts are issued for every payment and refund. |

## 5. Screens

1. Payment sheet (provider), with saved card option
2. Payment failed or provider unavailable
3. Refund on its way (in booking details)
4. Receipt (email and in-app)
5. Driver: set up payouts (opens the provider's onboarding) and account status
6. Driver: earnings (upcoming, paid, held; yearly statement)
7. Console: payments list and a booking's money timeline
8. Console: refund, hold and release
9. Console: chargebacks, reconciliation differences and monthly export

## 6. Data and API

Entities (see [domain model](../architecture/domain-model.md#payment)):

- `Payment`: booking, provider, provider reference, amount, currency, status
  (pending, succeeded, failed, refunded, partly refunded), card brand and last
  four digits, created and succeeded at.
- `Refund`: payment, amount, reason (rule M2 case or operator), status,
  provider reference, requested by.
- `PayoutAccount`: user, provider account reference, status.
- `Payout`: driver, amount, scheduled for, status (scheduled, held, paid,
  failed), provider reference, and the bookings it covers.
- `Chargeback`: payment, amount, reason, deadline, status, outcome.
- `LedgerEntry`: append-only; booking, type (payment, refund, payout, fee,
  chargeback), amount, currency, provider reference, created at.
- `ProviderEvent`: webhook event ID, type, received and processed at (for
  exactly-once processing).

Endpoints (`/v1`, shapes defined in `packages/contracts`):

| Method and path | Purpose |
| --------------- | ------- |
| `POST /bookings/{id}/payment` | Start or retry payment (spec 5) |
| `POST /webhooks/payments` | Signed events from the provider |
| `GET /me/payout-account` | Payout account status |
| `POST /me/payout-account/onboarding` | Link to the provider's hosted onboarding |
| `GET /me/earnings` | Upcoming, paid and held payouts |
| `GET /me/earnings/statements/{year}` | Yearly statement |
| `GET /bookings/{id}/receipts` | Receipts for a booking |

Console endpoints under `/admin/v1`: payments, a booking's money timeline,
refund, hold and release payouts, chargebacks, reconciliation and the monthly
export.

Jobs: payouts due (every 15 minutes), reconciliation (daily), retry failed
refunds and payouts.

Domain functions in `packages/domain`, free of I/O: the refund for a
cancellation (M2), which bookings a payout includes and when (M3), and the
ledger balance check.

Events: `PaymentSucceeded`, `PaymentFailed`, `RefundIssued`, `PayoutScheduled`,
`PayoutHeld`, `PayoutPaid`, `ChargebackOpened`.

## 7. Privacy and safety

- Card and bank details are handled only by the provider; we keep a token, the
  card brand and the last four digits. Our payment pages stay within the
  lightest PCI DSS scope.
- The provider checks drivers' identity for payouts and is a processor or
  separate controller under its own terms; we store only the account status.
  No kennitala is stored by ShareRide.
- Payment records, receipts and the ledger are kept as bookkeeping law
  requires (privacy inventory, **verify** period), even after an account is
  deleted, with only what the law needs.
- Webhooks are verified by signature; payment and console endpoints are
  covered by the penetration test before the beta.
- Payment amounts and references never appear in logs with names or contact
  details.

## 9. Testing

- **Unit (domain):** the refund for every cancellation case in rule M2,
  including exactly 15 minutes before boarding; which bookings a payout
  includes and when; held bookings; free bookings never create payments.
- **Property tests:** for any sequence of payment, refunds, payout and
  chargeback events, the ledger for a booking never pays out more than was
  paid in, and balances once settled.
- **Adapter contract tests:** the `PaymentProvider` adapter against a fake
  provider in CI, and against the provider's test mode nightly.
- **API integration:** webhooks verified, duplicated and out of order; a late
  confirmation is refunded; payouts held and released; reconciliation finds a
  planted difference.
- **End to end:** pay with the provider's test card and with Apple Pay and
  Google Pay test wallets, cancel in time and see the refund, complete a trip
  and see the payout scheduled, in Icelandic and English.
- **Manual:** a real low-value payment, refund and payout on production before
  the beta opens; receipt checked by the accountant.

## 10. Out of scope

A passenger service fee (from launch, [ADR 0014](../decisions/0014-passenger-service-fee-from-launch.md)), currencies other than ISK,
splitting one booking's payment between companions, tips, invoices for
companies, payouts to bank accounts outside Iceland (unless the provider
supports them at no extra effort), and cancellation flows themselves (spec 6).

# Privacy and GDPR

**Owner:** Martin · **Last reviewed:** 2026-11-23

> Working plan, not legal advice. The privacy policy and data protection
> impact assessment are reviewed by a lawyer before the beta.

## Framework

- The GDPR applies in Iceland through the EEA Agreement, together with the
  **Act on Data Protection and the Processing of Personal Data No. 90/2018**.
- The supervisory authority is **Persónuvernd** (the Icelandic Data
  Protection Authority).
- The PuffinRide company is the **controller**; until it is registered,
  Martin Safarcik is, as the published Privacy Policy says
  ([ADR 0043](../decisions/0043-legal-documents-published-from-the-repository.md)). Hosting, SMS, email, push,
  analytics, error tracking, identity and payment providers are
  **processors** (or separate controllers, for some identity and payment
  providers) and each needs a data processing agreement.
- All personal data stays in the EEA
  ([ADR 0007](../decisions/0007-eea-hosting-and-data-residency.md)), except
  what providers process elsewhere under a transfer basis, listed below,
  such as Google Analytics for visitors who accept
  ([ADR 0045](../decisions/0045-google-analytics-on-the-website-after-consent.md)).

## Principles we apply

1. **Collect less.** Last name, kennitala, home address and exact location are
   not needed and not collected.
2. **Show less.** Public profiles show first name, photo, bio, languages,
   badges and ratings, and the car's make, model and colour on trips. The
   number plate and exact pickup details are shown only to booked passengers. Phone numbers are never shown; people talk in the
   in-app chat.
3. **Keep less.** Every category has a retention period, enforced by scheduled
   jobs.
4. **Explain.** Plain-language privacy policy in Icelandic and English, and
   short explanations at the point of collection.
5. **Give control.** Export, correct and delete from the app without contacting
   support.

## Breaches

1. Contain the breach and preserve evidence.
2. Record it in the breach log, even if no notification is needed.
3. Notify Persónuvernd within 72 hours if it is likely to risk people's rights
   and freedoms.
4. Tell affected users without undue delay if the risk is high, with what
   happened and what they should do.
5. Write a post-incident note and fix the cause.

## Privacy in engineering

- Every table is listed in `packages/db/src/personal-data.ts` with the kind
  of personal data it holds, the part of "Download my data" it feeds, the
  columns that hold a person's ID, and what erasing an account does to its
  rows, column by column for rows that are kept. A test fails for a table,
  a person's ID column (a UUID column with a key to `users` or with none)
  or a text column of a kept row that is not listed. IDs inside JSON values
  are not checked, so tables keep a person's ID in a column of its own.
  What deletion does to each kind of data is in the domain
  (`deletionEffects`, spec 11, 3.5).
- No personal data in logs, analytics events, error reports or URLs.
  Google Analytics gets each screen's pattern, never its address
  ([spec 23](../specs/2026-11-22-google-analytics-and-search.md), R23.3), and
  loads only after the visitor accepts (R23.1).
- Search engines index only production's public pages that hold no
  person's data: Find, Carpool in Iceland, the meeting points, the places,
  About, the page on deleting an account, the legal texts and Cookies.
  Every other answer, and every answer off production, carries
  `X-Robots-Tag: noindex`; Find's rides leaving soon are kept out of
  snippets, and link previews name nobody
  ([spec 23](../specs/2026-11-22-google-analytics-and-search.md), R23.6,
  R23.7). A shared ride's link preview shows only what its trip page shows
  anyone with the link: the route, its meeting points, the day and the
  time; never a name, a photo, the car, a price or a seat count, since
  Facebook and other sites keep a copy of the card (spec 23, 3.10, R23.8).
  Sharing a ride on Facebook opens Facebook's own share window with the
  link only, after the person taps it: no Facebook script, SDK or pixel,
  and the post's text that PuffinRide writes holds no name, car, price or
  seat count ([spec 28](../specs/2026-11-25-share-a-ride.md), R28.2,
  R28.3). A ride's story picture for Instagram, which Instagram keeps a copy
  of once posted, shows the same as the link preview and nothing more
  (spec 28, 3.5).
- Production data never copied to staging or development.
- Privacy impact is a checkbox in every PR template.
- Retention jobs are tested like any other feature.

## Retention

Retention periods in the inventory are proposals. Final values are set with the
lawyer, then implemented as scheduled jobs and listed in the privacy policy.

The hourly clean-up runs every rule (`retentionRules` in the domain) and
records each run in `retention_runs` with its counts; `privacy runs` shows
the last run of each. A rule that fails is reported to Sentry and tried
again the next hour, while the others still run.

A hold (`privacy hold`) keeps what the clean-up would delete or make
anonymous about a person, a trip or a booking until its end: conversations,
ended sessions, the notification log, drafts, privacy requests, and the
account itself, which
is erased only once no hold covers the person, a trip they drove or rode
on, or a booking of theirs or on their trip. A hold on one person keeps
their conversations and the notifications about their bookings, but does
not stop the other people on those rides from being erased: hold the trip
or the booking to keep both sides. Codes, tokens, links and hashes are keys
rather than records, and an export is a copy of what is kept anyway: those
go on time. An account erased before a restore is erased again whatever
the restored copy holds.

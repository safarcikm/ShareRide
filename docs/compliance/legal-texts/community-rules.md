# ShareRide Community Rules (draft)

> **Draft for the lawyer, not in force.** Version 1, written 2026-11-16.
> See the [README](README.md).

**Last updated:** [date of publication]

ShareRide works because people who don't know each other share a car. These
rules keep that safe and fair. They are part of the
[Terms of Service](terms-of-service.md), and everyone accepts them when they
create an account.

## 1. Be who you say you are

- Use your real first name and a photo that shows your face clearly.
- Describe your car truthfully: make, model, colour and number plate.
- Declare only what is true, such as holding a valid driving licence or
  being a woman for women-only trips.
- Use one account, your own. Don't share it or let anyone else drive for you.

## 2. Keep it safe

### Drivers

- Drive legally and carefully, rested and sober. Never drive after drinking
  or taking drugs.
- Keep the car roadworthy and insured, with a seat belt for every passenger.
  Use winter tyres when the conditions call for them.
- Take only as many passengers as you have seats with seat belts.
- Check the weather and road conditions before you leave. If it isn't safe
  to drive, cancel and choose weather or road closed as the reason.

### Passengers

- Wear your seat belt, and respect the driver's reasonable safety
  instructions.
- Don't distract the driver.

### Everyone

- Meet at the meeting point of your booking, not somewhere else.
- Check the car, number plate and driver before you get in. You never have to
  ride if you don't feel safe.
- In an emergency, call 112 first. Then report it in the app.

## 3. Share costs, don't make a profit

- Drive trips you are making anyway. ShareRide is for sharing the cost of a
  trip, not for running a taxi or a business.
- Use the price the app works out. Never ask for extra money, tips or
  fuel money on top.
- Pay and get paid only the way the app says. Until payments in the app
  start, the app may say to pay the driver in person: then pay exactly the
  price it shows, when you get in, in cash or by bank transfer. Drivers
  never ask for more, or for money for a cancelled booking or a passenger
  who didn't come. Otherwise, never pay or accept cash or a bank transfer
  for a ShareRide ride, and never offer to.

## 4. Be reliable

- Turn up on time. A driver waits at least 10 minutes after the planned time
  at your stop.
- If your plans change, cancel as early as you can, so others can find
  another seat or passenger.
- Drivers: cancel a trip only when you have to, and choose the true reason.
- Don't mark a passenger as not having come, or report that a driver didn't
  come, unless it is true.

## 5. Be respectful

- Treat everyone with courtesy, in the car, in messages and in reviews.
- No harassment, threats, insults, or unwanted advances or contact, during
  or after a trip.
- No discrimination because of someone's origin, nationality, skin colour,
  religion, disability, age, sexual orientation, gender identity or gender.
  Women-only trips are the one exception, as described in section 7.
- Drivers: decide on requests fairly. Don't decline people because of who
  they are.
- Respect the agreed preferences of the trip, such as no smoking or no pets.

## 6. Messages, profiles and reviews

- Use messages for the trip. Don't send advertising, spam or links to
  scams.
- Keep contact details out of requests, trip notes and reviews; the app
  refuses them. Share your phone number in the chat only if you
  want to.
- Write honest reviews about the trip and the person. No insults, no
  personal details about others, and no reviews written to get back at
  someone.
- Nothing illegal, sexual, violent, hateful or misleading on your profile,
  in trip notes or in messages.

## 7. Women-only trips

- Turn on women-only trips only if you are a woman.
- On a women-only trip, every passenger must be a woman, including people
  you book for. Drivers don't bring anyone else along.
- Never ask anyone to prove they are a woman, and never judge it by how
  someone looks or sounds. If something is wrong, report it.

## 8. What happens if the rules are broken

You can report a person, trip, message, review or booking problem from the
app. We answer every report within 24 hours, and we never tell the reported
person who reported them.

Depending on how serious it is, we may:

- warn you;
- hide a message, review, trip note, profile photo or bio;
- withdraw your access to women-only trips;
- cancel your trips or bookings, with full refunds to the other people;
- suspend your account for a time or for good.

We tell you what we did and why, and which rule it concerns, by email. You can
appeal by replying to that email within 14 days, and a person will look at it
again.

## 2. What ShareRide is, and what it is not

1. ShareRide helps people who are already making a trip by car share the
   empty seats, and the cost of the trip, with others going the same way.
   Drivers publish a trip they are making anyway, from fixed meeting points
   on their route. Passengers book seats on all of the trip or part of it.
2. **We are an intermediary.** We provide the app, show trips, handle
   bookings and arrange payment through our payment provider. We do not
   carry passengers, we do not own or operate vehicles, and drivers do not
   work for us. The ride itself is an agreement between the driver and the
   passenger.
3. **Cost sharing, not a taxi.** Drivers share the cost of a trip; they do
   not earn a living from it. The app suggests prices from the distance and
   caps them, so that a full car does not bring the driver more than the
   cost of driving. Using ShareRide as a business, as a taxi or to make a
   profit is not allowed.
4. **We are not an emergency service.** In an emergency, call 112.
5. We do not check drivers, passengers, cars or licences before they use the
   service, apart from verifying each person's phone number. We rely on what
   people declare, on reviews and reliability figures, and on reports.

## 6. Prices and payment

1. **Prices.** Each stretch between two stops has its own price per seat,
   worked out by the app from the distance and the driver's price level. The
   price of a booking is the number of seats times the prices of the
   stretches it covers. You see the full price before you book. Prices are
   in Icelandic krónur.
2. **Paying in the app only.** All payments for rides go through the app.
   Never pay a driver in cash or by bank transfer for a ShareRide booking,
   and never ask a passenger to.
3. **When you pay.** In instant booking you pay at once. When the driver
   approves requests, you pay once the driver accepts. You have 30 minutes
   to pay, and never later than 15 minutes before the planned time at your
   boarding stop; until then your seats are held. A booking is confirmed
   only when it is paid. A free ride is confirmed at once.
4. **Our payment provider.** Payments are handled by [payment provider],
   which holds the money under its own licence and pays it out to the
   driver. We never see or store your card details or a driver's bank
   account details. [The terms of the provider that apply to you: link.]
5. **Payouts to drivers.** The driver is paid 24 hours after the trip
   completes, to the bank account they set up with the payment provider,
   unless a passenger has reported a problem with that booking. A reported
   problem holds that booking's payout until we decide.
6. **Fees.** During the beta there is no service fee, and we pay the payment
   provider's fees. Passengers pay the booking price, and drivers receive all
   of it. If we add a service fee later, it will be shown separately before
   you book, and we will tell you before it starts.
7. **Receipts.** You get a receipt for every payment and every refund in the
   app.
8. **Chargebacks.** If a passenger's bank reverses a payment after the driver
   was paid, and the trip did not happen because of the driver, the amount
   can be taken from the driver's later payouts.

## 7. Requests, changes and cancellations

1. **Requests.** When the driver approves bookings, your request holds the
   seats while the driver decides. It ends if the driver does not answer
   within 12 hours, or by 2 hours before your boarding time, whichever is
   earlier. You can withdraw a request at any time at no cost.
2. **The passenger cancels.**
   - Up to 15 minutes before the planned time at your boarding stop: full
     refund.
   - Later than that: no refund, and the driver is paid.
3. **The driver cancels** a booking or the whole trip: the passenger gets a
   full refund, always. This includes cancellations for weather or a closed
   road.
4. **The driver changes the trip.** The driver may move the departure by up
   to 2 hours, add stops, or change other details. If the departure moves by
   more than 30 minutes, you may cancel with a full refund at any time before
   the new departure. Your price never changes after you book. A stop where
   a passenger gets in or out cannot be removed.
5. **Refunds** go back to the card or payment method you paid with. How long
   the refund takes to show depends on your bank.
6. We may cancel a trip or a booking ourselves when these terms or the
   Community Rules are broken, or for safety. When we do, the other people
   on the trip get a full refund.

## 8. The trip day

1. Both sides can tap "I'm here" at the meeting point. The app records only
   the time, not your location.
2. **A passenger who doesn't come.** The driver waits at least 10 minutes
   after the planned time at the passenger's stop. After that, a driver who
   is there can mark the passenger as not having come. The passenger then
   gets no refund, and the driver is paid.
3. **A driver who doesn't come.** A passenger can report it from the end of
   the wait until 24 hours after the trip completes. If the report stands,
   the passenger gets a full refund.
4. **Disagreements.** The other side has 24 hours to contest a report. If
   nobody contests it, it stands. If it is contested, the payout is held and
   we decide, from the records in the app and what both sides tell us. Our
   decision is final in the app; it does not limit your rights under the law.
5. A trip completes on its own 2 hours after the planned time at its last
   stop.

## 9. Reviews and reliability

1. After a completed trip, the driver and each passenger can review each
   other within 14 days: a rating from 1 to 5 and a short comment. Reviews
   are shown when both have written one, or after 14 days, and cannot be
   changed once sent. Reviews must be honest and follow the Community Rules.
2. Your profile shows a **reliability figure**: the share of your trips and
   bookings over the last 12 months that you kept, once at least 5 count.
   Late cancellations by passengers (less than 24 hours before boarding),
   cancellations by drivers and not turning up count against it.
   Cancellations for weather or a closed road do not, unless the reason was
   clearly wrong, and neither does a passenger's cancellation after the
   driver moved the departure by more than 30 minutes.
3. A low reliability figure can lead to a warning or a suspension, decided by
   a person, never automatically.

## 11. Safety, reports and blocking

1. Meet only at the meeting point of your booking. Check that the car,

3. You can block another person. Neither of you will then see or book the
   other's trips, and your conversations become read-only. Blocking does not

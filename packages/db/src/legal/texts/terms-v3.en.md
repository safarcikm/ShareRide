## 2. What PuffinRide is, and what it is not

1. PuffinRide helps people who are already making a trip by car share the empty seats, and the cost of the trip, with others going the same way. Drivers publish a trip they are making anyway, from fixed meeting points on their route. Passengers book seats on all of the trip or part of it.

2. **We are an intermediary.** We provide the app, show trips and handle bookings. We do not carry passengers, we do not own or operate vehicles, and drivers do not work for us. The ride itself is an agreement between the driver and the passenger.

3. **Cost sharing, not a taxi.** Drivers share the cost of a trip; they do not earn a living from it. The app suggests prices from the distance and caps them, so that a trip never brings the driver more than the passengers' share of the cost of driving it. Using PuffinRide as a business, as a taxi or to make a profit is not allowed.

4. **We are not an emergency service.** In an emergency, call 112.

5. We do not check drivers, passengers, cars or licences before they use the service, apart from verifying each person's phone number. We rely on what people declare, on reviews and reliability figures, and on reports.

## 6. Prices and payment

1. **Prices.** Each stretch between two stops has its own price per seat, worked out by the app from the distance and the driver's price level. The price of a booking is the number of seats times the prices of the stretches it covers. You see the full price before you book. Prices are in Icelandic krónur.

2. **Paying the driver in person.** Payments in the app have not started yet. You pay the driver the price the app showed for your booking, when you get in, in cash or by bank transfer as you agree. Never pay more, and drivers never ask for more, or for money for a cancelled booking or for a passenger who didn't come.

3. **Confirmed bookings.** With instant booking, your booking is confirmed at once. When the driver approves requests, it is confirmed as soon as the driver accepts it.

4. **We do not handle the money.** We do not take, hold or pass on what passengers pay drivers, so we cannot refund it or give a receipt for it.

5. **Fees.** During the beta there is no service fee. If we add payments in the app or a service fee later, we will change these terms, and the app will ask you to accept the new version first.

## 7. Requests, changes and cancellations

1. **Requests.** When the driver approves bookings, your request holds the seats while the driver decides. It ends if the driver does not answer within 12 hours, or by 2 hours before your boarding time, whichever is earlier. You can withdraw a request at any time.

2. **Cancelling.** A passenger can cancel a booking, and a driver can cancel a booking or the whole trip, in the app. As nothing is paid in the app, nothing is refunded, and the passenger owes the driver nothing for a cancelled booking. Late cancellations count on reliability (section 9).

3. **The driver changes the trip.** The driver may move the departure by up to 2 hours, add stops, or change other details. If the departure moves by more than 30 minutes, you may cancel at any time before the new departure, and it does not count against your reliability. Your price never changes after you book. A stop where a passenger gets in or out cannot be removed.

4. We may cancel a trip or a booking ourselves when these terms or the Community Rules are broken, or for safety.

## 8. The trip day

1. Both sides can tap "I'm here" at the meeting point. The app records only the time, not your location.

2. **A passenger who doesn't come.** The driver waits at least 10 minutes after the planned time at the passenger's stop. After that, a driver who is there can mark the passenger as not having come. The passenger owes the driver nothing, and the no-show counts on their reliability.

3. **A driver who doesn't come.** A passenger can report it from the end of the wait until 24 hours after the trip completes. If the report stands, it counts on the driver's reliability.

4. **Disagreements.** The other side has 24 hours to contest a report. If nobody contests it, it stands. If it is contested, we decide, from the records in the app and what both sides tell us. Our decision is final in the app; it does not limit your rights under the law.

5. A trip completes on its own 2 hours after the planned time at its last stop.

## 9. Reviews and reliability

1. After a completed trip, the driver and each passenger can review each other within 14 days: a rating from 1 to 5 and a short comment. Reviews are shown when both have written one, or after 14 days, and cannot be changed once sent. Reviews must be honest and follow the Community Rules.

2. Your profile shows a **reliability figure**: the share of your trips and bookings over the last 12 months that you kept, once at least 5 count. Late cancellations by passengers (less than 24 hours before boarding), cancellations by drivers and not turning up count against it. Cancellations for weather or a closed road do not, unless the reason was clearly wrong, and neither does a passenger's cancellation after the driver moved the departure by more than 30 minutes.

3. A low reliability figure can lead to a warning or a suspension, decided by a person, never automatically.

## 11. Safety, reports and blocking

1. Meet only at the meeting point of your booking. Check that the car, number plate and driver match the booking before you get in. You never have to get into a car you do not feel safe in. If you don't ride for a safety reason, report it.

2. You can report a person, trip, message or review, or a problem with a booking, from the app. We answer every report within 24 hours. We never tell the person you reported who reported them.

3. You can block another person. Neither of you will then see or book the other's trips, and your conversations become read-only. Blocking does not change the rules for a booking you already have.

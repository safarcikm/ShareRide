PuffinRide works because people who don't know each other share a car. These rules keep that safe and fair. They are part of the Terms of Service, and everyone accepts them when they create an account.

## 1. Be who you say you are

- Use your real first name and a photo that shows your face clearly.
- Describe your car truthfully: make, model, colour and number plate.
- Declare only what is true, such as holding a valid driving licence.
- Use one account, your own. Don't share it or let anyone else drive for you.

## 2. Keep it safe

**Drivers**

- Drive legally and carefully, rested and sober. Never drive after drinking or taking drugs.
- Keep the car roadworthy and insured, with a seat belt for every passenger. Use winter tyres when the conditions call for them.
- Take only as many passengers as you have seats with seat belts.
- Check the weather and road conditions before you leave. If it isn't safe to drive, cancel and choose weather or road closed as the reason.

**Passengers**

- Wear your seat belt, and respect the driver's reasonable safety instructions.
- Don't distract the driver.

**Everyone**

- Meet at the meeting point of your booking, not somewhere else.
- Check the car, number plate and driver before you get in. You never have to ride if you don't feel safe.
- In an emergency, call 112 first. Then report it in the app.

## 3. Share costs, don't make a profit

- Drive trips you are making anyway. PuffinRide is for sharing the cost of a trip, not for running a taxi or a business.
- Use the price the app works out. Never ask for extra money, tips or fuel money on top.
- Payments in the app have not started yet. Pay the driver exactly the price the app shows, when you get in, in cash or by bank transfer. Drivers never ask for more, or for money for a cancelled booking or a passenger who didn't come.

## 4. Be reliable

- Turn up on time. A driver waits at least 10 minutes after the planned time at your stop.
- If your plans change, cancel as early as you can, so others can find another seat or passenger.
- Drivers: cancel a trip only when you have to, and choose the true reason.
- Don't mark a passenger as not having come, or report that a driver didn't come, unless it is true.

## 5. Be respectful

- Treat everyone with courtesy, in the car, in messages and in reviews.
- No harassment, threats, insults, or unwanted advances or contact, during or after a trip.
- No discrimination because of someone's origin, nationality, skin colour, religion, disability, age, sexual orientation, gender identity or gender.
- Drivers: decide on requests fairly. Don't decline people because of who they are.
- Respect the agreed preferences of the trip, such as no smoking or no pets.

## 6. Messages, profiles and reviews

- Use messages for the trip. Don't send advertising, spam or links to scams.
- Keep contact details out of requests, trip notes and reviews; the app refuses them. Share your phone number in the chat only if you want to.
- Write honest reviews about the trip and the person. No insults, no personal details about others, and no reviews written to get back at someone.
- Nothing illegal, sexual, violent, hateful or misleading on your profile, in trip notes or in messages.

## 7. What happens if the rules are broken

You can report a person, trip, message, review or booking problem from the app. We answer every report within 24 hours, and we never tell the reported person who reported them.

Depending on how serious it is, we may:

- warn you;
- hide a message, review, trip note, profile photo or bio;
- cancel your trips or bookings;
- suspend your account for a time or for good.

We tell you what we did and why, and which rule it concerns, in the app under Notices, and by email if you have added one. You can appeal there once, within 14 days, and a person will look at it again.

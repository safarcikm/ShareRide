## 1. Who we are

Martin Safarcik, Skinnar, 851 Hella, Iceland ("PuffinRide", "we"), is responsible for your personal data in the PuffinRide app and website (the controller). Questions about your data: admin@puffinride.is, or "Ask about my data" in Profile, under Privacy and your data.

## 2. The short version

- We collect what the service needs to work, and little else. We never ask for your kennitala, last name, home address or date of birth.
- Other people see your first name, photo, bio, languages, badges, reviews and reliability figure. They never see your phone number.
- Your data is stored in the European Economic Area (EEA).
- We do not sell your data or show ads. On the website, we count visits with Google Analytics only if you accept, and you can turn it off at any time. The phone apps count no visits, and there is no tracking in our emails.
- You can download your data and delete your account in the app, at any time.

## 3. What we collect, why, and for how long

For each kind of data: what it is, why we need it, the legal basis, and how long we keep it.

**Account.** Your phone number, first name, language, your confirmation that you are 18 or older (yes and the date), and your email address once you verify it. To create your account, sign you in and contact you. Basis: contract. Kept while you have an account, then until 7 days after you ask to delete it.

**Sign-in records.** Sign-in codes and limits, stored only in a form that cannot be read back, and your signed-in devices (name, platform, app version, times). If you set a password, we keep it only in a form that cannot be read back, never the password itself, and when you set it. If you set up Face ID or fingerprint, your device makes a passkey for PuffinRide, and we keep for each device: the passkey's ID and public key; a few technical details, such as whether the passkey is backed up; the browser and system it was set up in; and when it was set up and last used. Your face or fingerprint never leaves your device and never reaches us. After you sign in with a password, we ask your browser to save such a passkey if it can do so by itself; when one is saved, we email you. To check it is you, keep you signed in, and stop fraud. Basis: contract, and our legitimate interest in stopping fraud. Codes until used or expired; limits 24 hours; devices until you sign out, or after 60 days unused, and the record of a sign-in that ended for 30 days more; a password and Face ID or fingerprint until you remove them in Profile, under Sign-in methods, or until your account is erased.

**Legal acceptances.** Which version of the terms and rules you accepted, when, and in which language. To show what you agreed to. Basis: contract and legal obligation. Kept while you have an account.

**Profile.** Your photo, bio, languages and badges. So people know who they travel with. Basis: contract. Kept while you have an account.

**Car.** Make, model, colour and number plate. So passengers recognise the car. Basis: contract. Kept until you remove the car, and with the trips it was used for.

**Driving licence declaration.** The date you declared you hold a valid licence; no licence number or copy. To let you publish trips. Basis: contract. Kept while you have an account.

**Trips and bookings.** Meeting points, times, seats, prices, requests and their messages, changes and cancellations with their reasons, and that the passenger pays the driver in person. We never take or see that payment. To provide the service and apply its rules. Basis: contract. Kept 3 years after the trip, then made anonymous.

**Messages** between a driver and a passenger. To arrange the trip. Basis: contract. Kept 12 months after the trip, unless a report needs them.

**Trip day records.** "I'm here" times (no location), reports that someone didn't come, and what each side said. To tell the other side you are at the meeting point, and to settle disagreements. Basis: contract and legitimate interest. Kept with the booking or, for a driver's "I'm here", with the trip.

**Reviews and reliability.** For trust between people. Basis: contract and legitimate interest. Reviews are kept while the person reviewed has an account; reliability covers 12 months.

**Reports, blocks and moderation.** What was reported, our actions, statements of reasons and appeals. For safety. Basis: legitimate interest, and legal obligation where it applies. Kept 3 years after the report is closed; blocks until you unblock.

**Notifications.** Your push token, and a log of what kind of notification was sent and whether it arrived, without its text. To reach you, and to check that people were told. Basis: contract and legitimate interest. The push token while you are signed in on that phone; the log 90 days.

**Feedback** you send in the beta, with an optional screenshot and technical details. To improve the app. Basis: legitimate interest. Kept 12 months after the beta ends, or until you delete your account.

**Technical logs and error reports,** with personal details removed. For security and to fix errors. Basis: legitimate interest. Kept 30 days.

**Visits to the website,** only if you accept Google Analytics there. Which screens you open, named only by their kind, such as "a trip page", never which trip, person or search; the site you came from, by its address without the page; which of our ads or flyers brought you, if one did; your browser, system, screen size and language; and roughly where you are, from your network address, which Google says it does not keep. Google links your visits with a random ID in its cookies, never with your account, name, phone number or email address. To see how many people visit and which screens they use. Basis: consent, which you can withdraw at any time on the Cookies page. Google keeps it 14 months; its cookies last 13 months, and a visit does not extend them. We see only totals. Advertising features are off, and Google does not use it for ads.

**Privacy requests** and our answers. To answer your requests about your rights. Basis: legal obligation. Kept 3 years after the answer.

We do not store your location. When you ask the app to find meeting points near you, your phone's location is used for that search only.

If your account was suspended and then deleted, we keep a one-way hash of your phone number until the suspension would have ended, and at most 3 years, so the number cannot be used to sign up again.

## 4. Who can see what

- **Anyone using PuffinRide:** your first name, photo, bio, languages, badges, reviews and reliability figure. For a trip you publish, also its route, times, prices, free seats, notes and your car's make, model and colour.
- **People you travel with:** once a booking is confirmed, the driver and the passenger see each other's profile, the exact meeting details and the car's number plate, and can message each other.
- **Nobody else** sees your phone number, email address or the messages in your conversations.
- **PuffinRide's operator** can see what the service needs to run it and keep it safe. The operator reads a conversation only when a report or a trip-day disagreement covers it, and every such look is logged.

## 5. Who we share it with

We use these service providers. Each one processes data for us under a data processing agreement, only as we instruct.

- **Render,** with Cloudflare for the connection: hosting the app, the database and logs. Frankfurt, Germany.
- **Vonage:** sending SMS, such as codes that check your phone number and last-minute trip changes. Stored in Ireland and Germany; may be processed elsewhere for support and fraud checks.
- **Mailjet:** sending email, such as sign-in codes and booking updates. Germany and Belgium.
- **Expo, with Apple and Google:** sending push notifications to the PuffinRide app on your phone. United States.
- **Expo (app updates):** when the PuffinRide app opens on your phone, it asks Expo for a newer version of its own code, sending a random ID the app makes when it is installed (not linked to your account), the app's version and which update it runs, the phone's system and, after a start that failed, the error's message. United States.
- **Sentry:** error reports, with personal details removed. Frankfurt, Germany.
- **Hetzner:** storing profile and meeting point photos. Nuremberg, Germany.
- **Google (Google Analytics),** only if you accept on the website: counting visits. Google Ireland, with Google in the United States.

If you sign in with Google or Apple, you do so on their own page, under their own privacy policies, and they learn that you sign in to PuffinRide. They share with us their ID for you, your email address and whether they have verified it, and your first name. We keep the ID and a verified email address while the Google or Apple account is linked to yours; your first name only fills in the form when you create your account. You can remove Google or Apple at any time in Profile, under Sign-in methods.

If you get the PuffinRide app from Google Play or the App Store, Google or Apple handle the download, any rating or review you leave in their store, and the crash reports they collect if you allow it, under their own terms and privacy policies, and they show us those ratings, reviews and crash reports. If you test the app before it is released, through Apple's TestFlight or Google Play testing, which Apple and Google run under their own terms, we give Apple or Google your email address to invite you, and they show us how the test version works for you: installs, crashes, your phone's model and system, and the feedback you send, with any screenshot you add.

We share data with the police or other authorities only when the law requires it, or to protect someone's life or safety.

## 6. Data outside the EEA

We store your data in the EEA. A few providers may process some of it outside the EEA: Expo, Apple and Google for push notifications, Expo for app updates, Apple and Google for testing the app before release, Google for counting visits to the website if you accept, and Vonage for support and fraud checks. When that happens, the transfer rests on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified under it, and otherwise on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses in our agreement with the provider.

## 7. Your rights

You can:

- **Download your data:** "Download my data" in Profile, under Privacy and your data, gives you a file with the data we hold about you, usually within minutes, kept for 7 days. It never reveals who reported you or another person's details beyond their first name.
- **Correct it:** edit your profile in the app, or ask us for anything else.
- **Delete your account:** "Delete my account" in Profile, under Privacy and your data. Upcoming trips and bookings are cancelled under the normal rules first. After 7 days, in which you can change your mind, we erase your personal data or make it anonymous. Reviews you wrote stay, shown as written by "a former member". We keep only what the law or safety requires, for the periods above.
- **Object or ask us to restrict** how we use your data, where the law gives you that right.
- **Withdraw your consent** to Google Analytics: turn it off on the Cookies page, under About PuffinRide. Counting stops at once, and Google's cookies are removed from your browser.
- **Complain** to Persónuvernd, the Icelandic Data Protection Authority (www.personuvernd.is).

We answer requests within one month. Write to admin@puffinride.is or use "Ask about my data" in Profile, under Privacy and your data.

## 8. Storage on your device

The app and website store only what they need to work: what keeps you signed in, which is sent to us with each request, and what only helps the app on your device, such as the way you signed in last time, your last searches and a booking you started before signing up, which stays on your device and is not sent to us. None of this needs consent. If you set up Face ID or fingerprint, your device or your password manager keeps the passkey, and may copy it to your other devices under its own terms; we hold only what section 3 lists. Removing it in Profile removes what we hold; your device may keep its own copy, which then no longer signs you in. The phone app also keeps the random ID it sends Expo when it checks for an update (section 5). On the website, Google Analytics sets its cookies only if you accept (section 3). Your answer is kept on your device for 12 months, then we ask again; you can change it at any time on the Cookies page. We use no advertising cookies.

## 9. Age

PuffinRide is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data about children.

## 10. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit, access limited to those who need it, and a log of every action the operator takes. If a breach puts your rights at high risk, we will tell you without undue delay, and we will notify Persónuvernd within 72 hours where the law requires it.

## 11. Changes

When this policy changes in a way that matters to you, we will tell you in the app before the change applies. The version number at the top shows the version you are reading.
